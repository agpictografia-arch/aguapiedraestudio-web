Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026.

This policy applies to Aguapiedra Estudio, its apps, and this website. It is published under the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar US state laws, including those of Nevada and Connecticut.

Consumer health data we collect: none. Our apps store any health information you enter only on your own device, and in Apple Health if you allow it. That information is never sent to us, and we cannot see it.

What stays on your device: depending on the app and on what you choose to use, this can include menstrual cycle and bleeding dates, symptoms, mood, energy, birth control, year of birth, and menopause stage. None of it is sent to us.

Sources: none, because we do not collect consumer health data.

Purposes: none, because we do not collect consumer health data.

Sharing and sale: we do not share or sell consumer health data. No third parties or affiliates receive consumer health data from us.

This website: it uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers, and it has no forms. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site. We do not receive or use that data.

Location: our apps do not use your location, and we do not use geofencing.

Your rights: you can ask us to confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, to access it, to delete it, or to withdraw consent. Because we hold none, we will confirm that in writing. Email support@aguapiedraestudio.app. If you disagree with our answer, reply with “Appeal” in the subject line and we will review it.

Changes: any change is posted on this page with its date. If any of our apps ever starts collecting consumer health data, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before it happens.

Who is responsible. Aguapiedra Estudio. Contact: support@aguapiedraestudio.app
